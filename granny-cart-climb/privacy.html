Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Granny Cart Climb (“the game”) uses and why. The game is made by an independent developer (GitHub: naradharanindra12-create), referred to below as “we”.

Short version: the game has no accounts and we don't collect or receive your personal information. Your progress is saved only on your phone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and purchases by Google Play, and Google handles the data for those services under its own privacy policy.

1. Information stored on your device

To keep your climb safe (for example if you get a phone call), the game saves on your device:

  • your game progress: grandma's position on the mountain, the climb timer and any checkpoint;
  • statistics: best height, distance fallen, number of summits, best times;
  • settings (sound, music, vibration, controls) and which cosmetic items you own or have equipped;
  • pictures you create with the share or screenshot buttons.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads appear between climbs, and optionally when you choose to watch a video to place a checkpoint. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived;
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (for example views and taps), and diagnostic data.

Depending on your choices and where you live, Google may use this information to show personalized ads. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some phones). Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Buying “Remove Ads” in the game stops the ads that appear between climbs.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases (cosmetic items and “Remove Ads”) are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card or billing details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google Play (the product that was bought and a purchase token) so it can unlock the item on your device. Google Play's handling of your data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing

When you tap a share button, the game creates a picture and/or a short text (for example “I fell 4,382 m today”) and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it. Whatever you share is then handled by the app you choose.

5. Leaderboard

The leaderboard in this version works on your device only. It shows example players and your own best times, and no scores are sent anywhere. If an online leaderboard is added in the future, this policy will be updated first.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to talk to Google Play for purchases.
  • Vibration: for the optional vibration effects (you can turn them off in the game's settings).

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help.

8. Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold any personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can delete everything the game stored on your device by clearing its data or uninstalling it. For data processed by Google for ads or purchases, you can use Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact
